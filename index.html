FarePath transit data

The transit dataset behind the FarePath Android app — Mumbai rail stations, lines and fares — published under ODbL 1.0, together with the full pipeline that produces it.

This exists for two reasons. The dataset is derived from OpenStreetMap, which makes it a Derivative Database under ODbL, and share-alike requires it be offered to anyone who received it. And a fare app should be auditable: if the app says a journey costs ₹40, you should be able to see where that number came from.

  • Repository and rebuild instructions
  • transit.json — 218 stations, 19 services, 6 operators
  • layout.svg — the schematic map
  • FarePath privacy policy

Fare confidence is recorded per operator rather than assumed. Metro Line 3 and the Monorail station-pair matrix are verified against operator sources; the rest are marked estimated. The suburban second-class table is the largest known gap — Indian Railways' official fare PDFs refuse connections from outside India, so the primary chart could not be retrieved. First class and AC fares are omitted rather than guessed. Corrections with a source are very welcome.
